Wrist Fracture Morphology

Let's see how you classify and treat certain fractures.

You will be given a radiographic image of a wrist fracture. For each image, we ask that you select how you would name the fracture, as well as what treatment method you would use. Don't overthink it — answer efficiently and as quickly as you can.

Research Participation Information

Study Title

Wrist Fracture Morphology Study (CL-IOH SCORE 2026)

Principal Investigator

Soroush Baghdadi, MD - UCLA Department of Orthopedics

Purpose

This survey will determine whether orthopedic healthcare professionals can reach agreement regarding terminology and treatment methods. You will be asked to complete this survey twice, with the assistance of an educational pamphlet provided between survey administrations, over the span of two weeks.

What You'll Do

You will answer the prompted questions for a set of __ X-rays. Once complete, you will receive an educational pamphlet regarding wrist fracture management. We ask that you read through the pamphlet thoroughly. In two weeks, you will be asked to complete the same survey a second time.

Voluntary Participation

Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may stop at any time without consequence. You may also choose to participate anonymously.

Data Protection

  • Your clinical opinions, not personal health information, are collected.
  • Responses are stored on a secure, access-controlled platform.
  • Your name is collected solely for delivering personalized results and is stored separately from survey responses.
  • Published results will use only aggregate, de-identified data.
  • Anonymous participants will not have identifying data collected.

How Results Will Be Used

Aggregate results may be published in peer-reviewed journals. Individual results will be shared only with you through a personalized report.
